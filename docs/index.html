Connors RSI-2 (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/CHF, USD/JPY, EUR/JPY)

At an hourly close between 07:00 and 16:00 NY: BUY when price is above its 200-hour average, RSI(2) is below 5 and ADX is above 25. SELL is the mirror image. TP = 75% of the way to the 5-hour average. SL = 2× hourly ATR. Set both in MT4 and leave the trade alone. Backtest (9 months, after costs): about 86–89% winners.

NZD/USD · TEST (paper only until it passes)

H1 above the 200-hour average, RSI(2) below 15, Stochastic(14) in the bottom 15%, and a bullish 5-minute candle at the hourly close, NY 07–16. TP = $3 net at 0.03 lot, SL = 4× the TP, close by hand after 24h. SELL is the mirror image.

EUR/CHF · TEST (paper only until it passes)

H1 Stochastic(14,3,3) crosses up below 20 (or down above 80), ADX above 25, and Bollinger width in its top 30%, NY 07–16. TP = 0.5× hourly ATR, SL = 2× ATR, close by hand after 72h.

How TEST pairs pass

They show T while testing. After 15+ live paper trades with 80%+ wins and a profit, the grade becomes a letter (A/B/C), the badge says PASSED, and you get a phone alert. If one is under 70% wins or losing money after 10+ trades, it shows D · failing, and you should switch it off in config.json.
